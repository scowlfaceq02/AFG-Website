Performance Reporting Coming Soon

Axiom Forecasting Group is committed to transparent and objective evaluation of forecast accuracy. Performance statistics will be published after a sufficient number of forecasts have reached resolution.

AFG does not currently publish performance statistics because there is not yet sufficient resolved forecast history to produce meaningful evaluation metrics.

Once enough historical data has been collected, this page will include:

  • Brier Score performance
  • Forecast calibration analysis
  • Category-level accuracy
  • Conviction tier performance
  • Historical forecast archive
  • Comparison against relevant forecasting benchmarks

Until then, AFG's primary focus is producing disciplined, evidence-based probability forecasts while building a transparent historical record of predictions.
